Systematic Methodology

The 4 Pillars of TSS Quranic Memorization

A proven, rigorous structure designed to ensure long-term retention, confident recitation, and zero cognitive fatigue for young students.

1. Sabaq سبق

Daily New Verses: The student memorizes a tailored portion (from half to a full page) under direct teacher guidance, focusing on flawless pronunciation and initial memorization.

2. Sabqi سبقی

Recent Juz Revision: Daily recitation of the previous 1 to 2 Paras memorized in the past weeks, solidifying fresh memory and preventing early verse slippage.

3. Manzil / Daur منزل

Cumulative Retention: Regular cyclic revision of all previous Paras completed from the beginning of the Quran, ensuring the child retains every single Surah for life.

4. Tajweed & Tartil حسن تجوید

Makharij & Rules: In-depth training on Makharij al-Huroof, Ahkam of Nun & Mim Sakinah, Ghunnah, and Maddat, certified by recognized Qari educators.

Balanced Development

Integrated Tahfeez & Modern Schooling

Parents never have to compromise between religious memorization and contemporary academic advancement.

Quranic Tahfeez Track

A rigorous spiritual and intellectual curriculum focused on Quranic memorization, character formation, and Islamic manners (Adaab & Akhlaaq).

  • Individualized Sabaq listening every morning by certified Ustadh.
  • Daily Daur sessions with peer recitation and teacher auditing.
  • Daily Sunnah supplications (Masnoon Duain) & practical Salah training.
  • Official Tahfeez Certificate & Dastar-e-Fazeelat upon completion.

Integrated Academic Track

Daily core academic periods ensuring the student stays on track with standard Punjab Textbook Board and national learning standards.

  • English Comprehension, Reading, Grammar, and Vocabulary.
  • Fundamental Mathematics (Arithmetic, Fractions, Pre-Algebra).
  • Urdu Adab, writing practice, and General Science fundamentals.
  • Smooth, direct promotion to Middle School (6th–8th) or Matric after Hifz.
