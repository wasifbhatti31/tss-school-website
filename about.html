Welcome to TSS

Inspiring Minds, Building Character

Located at Chuburji Park, Old Rifle Range Road, Lahore, TSS is a premier educational institution dedicated to providing balanced academic excellence, scientific knowledge, and Islamic character building.

Founded on the philosophy of holistic development, we prepare students for Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Lahore examinations while embedding core Islamic moral values through our specialized Tahfeez-ul-Quran wing.
