Intermediate College Wing (F.Sc & ICS)

Affiliated with Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Lahore. Premier college streams in Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, and ICS Computer Science.

Higher Secondary Wing

Empowering College Students for Board Merit & University Admissions

The Intermediate College Wing at TSS Lahore provides an exceptional academic launchpad for students targeting top medical colleges, engineering universities, and premier computer science institutes across Pakistan (KEMU, AIMC, UET Lahore, NUST, FAST-NUCES, PUCIT, GIKI).

Our expert faculty blends comprehensive BISE Lahore syllabus coverage with conceptual depth required for competitive university entrance exams (MDCAT & ECAT), supported by cutting-edge science and IT laboratories.

College Academic Disciplines

Medical & Life Sciences

F.Sc Pre-Medical

The premier pathway for future Doctors, Dentists, Pharmacists, and Bio-Medical Scientists.

  • Biology (Botany, Zoology, Genetics & Physiology)
  • Physics (Modern Physics, Optics, Electromagnetism)
  • Chemistry (Organic, Inorganic & Physical Chemistry)
  • English (Prose, Poetry, Essays & Grammar)
  • Urdu, Islamic Studies & Pakistan Studies
  • Tarjamat-ul-Quran (Board Scheme)
  • MDCAT Concept Alignment
Engineering & Physical Sciences

F.Sc Pre-Engineering

Rigorous analytical training for aspiring Civil, Electrical, Mechanical & Software Engineers.

  • Mathematics (Calculus, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry)
  • Physics (Mechanics, Thermodynamics & Electronics)
  • Chemistry (Industrial & Experimental Chemistry)
  • English (Higher Composition & Board Literature)
  • Urdu, Islamic Studies & Pakistan Studies
  • Tarjamat-ul-Quran (Board Scheme)
  • ECAT & UET Prep Foundation
Computer Science & AI

ICS (Computer Science)

Modern technology education for future Software Developers, AI Engineers, and Data Scientists.

  • Computer Science (Data Structures, C/C++ Programming, Database MS Access)
  • Mathematics (Higher Algebra, Calculus & Logic)
  • Physics or Statistics (Choice based on career roadmap)
  • English (Business & Technical Writing)
  • Urdu, Islamic Studies & Pakistan Studies
  • Tarjamat-ul-Quran (Board Scheme)
  • FAST, NUST & PUCIT Entry Foundation
