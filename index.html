About THSS

Inspiring Minds, Building Futures

Founded with a commitment to holistic education and strong moral values, Taqwa Higher Secondary School nurtures curiosity, resilience, and leadership in every student.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to empower young minds with world-class education, critical thinking skills, and moral integrity to excel in an ever-changing global society.

We believe that every child possesses unique potential. Through personalized mentorship, innovative STEM & Humanities programs, and rich co-curricular opportunities, we inspire our students to explore their passions and make meaningful contributions to the world.
