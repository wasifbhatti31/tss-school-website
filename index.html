Welcome to Taqwa School

Inspiring Minds, Building Character

Located at Chuburji Park, Old Rifle Range Road, Lahore, Taqwa Higher Secondary School is a premier educational network institution dedicated to providing balanced academic excellence, scientific knowledge, and Islamic character building.

Our educational structure covers Hifz-e-Quran memorization, Middle School (Class 6th to 8th), and Board Matriculation & F.Sc / ICS (Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, and Computer Science) affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Lahore.
